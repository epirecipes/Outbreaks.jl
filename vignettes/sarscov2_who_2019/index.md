# SARS-CoV-2 WHO Situation Reports, 2019–2020


## Overview

This dataset contains information manually transcribed from the World
Health Organization (WHO) Situation Reports on SARS-CoV-2 (COVID-19)
during the early phase of the pandemic. Each row corresponds to a single
WHO Situation Report. Data availability varies across reports as
reporting formats evolved over time.

## Source

WHO Situation Reports:
<https://www.who.int/emergencies/diseases/novel-coronavirus-2019/situation-reports>
